How I work

The sloth method

Sloths aren't lazy – they're efficient. They never waste energy on the wrong move. Neither should your project.

Hang around first

Before writing a line of code I get to know your domain, your team and what actually needs solving.

Move deliberately

Small, tested steps that ship continuously. Steady progress beats heroic weekends every time.

Leave it tidy
