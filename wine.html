Theme ideas

Something to sip on

A few favourites for your after-work – or come with your own idea and we'll build an evening around it.

Tour de France

Six stages, six regions – from Champagne in the north to Provence in the south. No bike needed.

Big Bold Reds of Italy

Barolo, Amarone, Primitivo and friends. Dark, generous wines for people who like a firm handshake.

Six Shades of Riesling

Bone-dry to lusciously sweet, Germany to Australia – one grape, a surprising number of personalities.

Old World vs. New World

The same grapes from Europe and from the rest of the world, side by side. Can you tell which is which?

Bubbles Beyond Champagne

Cava, Crémant, Prosecco, English fizz – and one real Champagne hiding among them. Spot it if you can.

Blind Date

Six wines with the labels hidden. Guess the grape, the country and the price – in teams, with points.

Cheap vs. Expensive

Pairs of wines at very different prices, served blind. Can you taste the difference – and is it worth the money?

Good to know

Questions, answered slowly

What's the idea behind your tastings?

To take the snobbery out of wine. Wine is made to be enjoyed, there are no right answers and all taste is subjective. I'll share what's generally true about a wine and what I taste in the glass – but what you taste is just as valid. Most of all, I want everyone to have a fun night.

Where do the tastings take place?

At your office, your home or a venue of your choice in the Gothenburg area.

Who provides the wine?

I do. I pick the wines based on what you'd like – a favourite region, a grape you've always wanted to try, or simply "surprise us" – and bring everything to the tasting. The wine is included in the price.

Do we need to know anything about wine?

Not at all. The tastings are made for curious people, not experts. If there are enthusiasts in the group, they'll find something new too.

Can you combine it with food?

I don't provide food, but if you already have something planned I can adjust the wines to suit it, or suggest a few easy things to serve alongside.

How far in advance should we book?

A few weeks ahead is ideal, especially around Christmas and during the spring party season.

All participants must be of legal drinking age. Please drink responsibly.
